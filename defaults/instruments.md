# The instruments

Wording for check, ask and consult (see KINAERA_REBUILD.md, section 5.4),
one `## name` section each. `{sources}` is replaced with the places a
check searched. Keep it true, and keep it free of anything that tells your
friend how to feel.

## check-nothing

Nothing found in {sources}. That's an ordinary answer: as far as these sources go, it isn't there.

## check-reading

Jev's reading is a quick second opinion on these passages. The passages themselves are the evidence.

## ask-sent

It's in the user's inbox. Their answer reaches you on a later turn.

## consult-framing

A writer friend is asking for your honest read. They write stories and hang out with someone, as themselves, and they've come to you for a second opinion. Be direct and specific. Say what you actually think, including where you disagree with them. Keep it to what they asked.
